Finalverse
Mac developer preview · v0.4.0-preview.1

A world you can
make your own.

Explore, connect and create in a shared 3D world.
Your avatar. Your ideas. A place to bring them to life.

01 / CONNECT

Choose your world

Finalverse Harbor requires an invitation and your own account. Downloading the client does not register you. Select the intended grid before entering credentials.

02 / EXPLORE

Be there

Move with the arrow keys, look around with the camera controls and meet others through chat.

03 / CREATE

Shape your space

Where available, ask AI about your world, review a proposed change, approve it and inspect History or Undo. Your connected world retains permissions and authority.

Experimental developer preview; not a public-world launch. Hosted AI features depend on your world and account permissions.
Built on the open-source Second Life Viewer by Linden Lab and its contributors. Finalverse is an independent project.
