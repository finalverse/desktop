## Capture method

The designated loopback MutSea grid is `http://127.0.0.1:18094/`, region `a9d44538-26ca-4586-a8a9-e551722ffa15`. Eight bounded named cameras use region-local meters and FOV 1 radian. Each has 15 seconds of warm-up followed by 15 seconds of frame samples. The runner requires a foreground, visible, unminimized viewer; focus loss, resolution changes or cached-object-count changes reset the current sample. Builds finished before sampling. It moves only the local camera and optionally selects a local environment preview; it sends no avatar movement or world mutation.

PNG resolution is **1280 × 738 native raw pixels**, with UI/HUD excluded. Retina UI screenshots are a different resolution and are not the performance evidence. CSV intervals are CPU-observed frames, not GPU timestamps. FPS is total frames / total measured seconds; p95 is nearest-rank. Image encoding and camera transition time are excluded by the next warm-up.

## Authoring and compatibility

Studio now supplies nine original CC0 numerical material seeds and three original environment candidates. They contain no external texture/model payloads. Texture budgets are 2K hero, 1K standard, 512 background where sufficient. These are authoring seeds, not a production texture library. The current native importer supports untextured, unrigged legacy RGBA/fullbright; it does not yet deliver the PBR intent encoded in GLB materials.

Bench v0.1.3 was generated from the corrected color-space exporter: 13 parts, 1404 triangles, two materials, zero texture bytes. Package validation passed. Reading the actual GLB and Collada verified that both materials have linear glTF factors and sRGB legacy tint respectively. The new package is **not instantiated live**. Existing v0.1.1 live content and v0.1.2 export evidence were preserved.

Golden Hour, Day and Night specs are prepared for native local previews. They have not yet passed visual acceptance or authorized region publication/restart. The numerical presets retain separate provenance from inherited native assets. Emissive geometry is not a light source; a coherent night scene still needs actual path/interior lights.

Inherited avatar rigging, attachment, bake and motion infrastructure remains the proposed production path. Human outfit-color persistence was verified previously and gray-blue pants remain visible in this run. Lumi's rigged body, face, original hair/outfit, motion profile and template provisioning remain unresolved; no portrait is assumed legally redistributable.
